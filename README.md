# The WebSwan Sky Dream

An endless sky-road game starring the WebSwan. Glide down a road of blue tiles above the clouds, jump the gaps, and catch glowing feathers.

**Play:** https://taniad2169.github.io/The-WebSwan-Sky-Dream/

## How to play
- **iPhone & Android:** tap the big JUMP button (or tap the road). Hold ◀ ▶ or slide your finger to steer.
- **Computer:** Space, ↑ or click to jump. Arrow keys or the mouse to steer.
- Feathers are +25 points. The road speeds up the farther you go.

Made by The WebSwan. Inspired by *Skydreams* by Frank Force.
